## Brand & Style

The design system establishes a sanctuary-like digital environment engineered specifically for university students navigating stress, vulnerability, and academic pressure, while offering certified counselors an orderly, focused clinical workstation. 

### Brand Character & Emotional Resonance
- **Sanctuary & Safety:** Interaction patterns eliminate anxiety through predictable layouts, transparent data disclosures, and zero sudden layout shifts.
- **Warm Authority:** Balances institutional, clinical credibility with human-centered empathy, steering clear of cold hospital tropes and overly casual consumer tropes.
- **De-Stigmatization & Agency:** Interfaces celebrate privacy and user empowerment, elevating anonymity controls and calm reassurance at every decision point.

### Design Style: Soft Modern & Tactile Reassurance
The visual system blends **modern clinical clarity** with **tactile softness**. Surfaces rely on organic off-whites, botanical tones, delicate container borders, and airy spacing. Hard 90-degree corners and high-contrast brutalist outlines are entirely eliminated in favor of generous radii (16px to 24px) and soft, low-luminance ambient drops that mimic natural daylight falling on matte paper.

## Colors

The color palette prioritizes autonomic nervous system grounding through botanical greens, soothing lavenders, and glare-free warm neutrals.

### Palette Architecture
- **Primary — Deep Botanical Sage (`#2E7D5B`):** Represents grounded stability, natural renewal, and clinical reassurance. Paired with soft tint backgrounds (`#EBF5F0`) for supportive cards and selected states.
- **Secondary — Empathetic Lavender (`#7C69AF`):** Softens cognitive fatigue; applied across reflective check-ins, meditation exercises, and active consultation modes. Tint container is set to `#F3EFFF`.
- **Neutral — Slate Charcoal (`#1E293B`):** Retains high-contrast legibility for micro-typography, legal notices, and clinical notes without the harshness of pure black. Paired with muted slate (`#64748B`) for helper texts.
- **Background Canvas — Warm Parchment (`#FAFAF9`):** A soft, eye-resting matte ground that avoids digital glare across long nocturnal reading and journaling sessions.
- **Tertiary / Utility — Emergency SOS Coral (`#E11D48`):** Reserved exclusively for urgent crisis interventions, hotlines, and critical escalation triggers. Never used for standard destructive interface actions like "Delete note".

## Typography

The typography couples the welcoming, open aperture geometry of **Plus Jakarta Sans** for headers with the systematic, neutral legibility of **Inter** for all prose, notes, and session documentation.

### Hierarchy & Legibility Rules
- **Display & Section Headers:** Plus Jakarta Sans provides a friendly, human, non-clinical greeting. Headings maintain balanced letter-spacing to prevent aggressive optical density.
- **Body & Clinical Transcripts:** Inter guarantees high reading speed and zero visual ambiguity across varied digital screens, even at low brightness settings.
- **Anonymity Badges & Metadata:** Set in `label-sm` and `label-md` using medium-to-semibold weights with subtle tracking to provide effortless identification of counselor credentials and session states.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model is built on an intentional 8pt spatial grid with generous breathing room to counteract cognitive overload during moments of acute stress.

### Grid & Breakpoints
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column fluid layout with `margin: 1rem` and `gutter: 1rem`. Bottom sheets and unified single-column cards dominate the workspace.
- **Tablet (768px - 1024px):** 8-column layout with `margin: 1.5rem` and `gutter: 1.5rem`. Split views support concurrent tele-consultation video and self-reflection notes.
- **Desktop (> 1024px):** 12-column layout capped at a maximum content constraint of `1280px` centered on canvas. Sidebar navigation is static and unobtrusive.

### Spacing Philosophy
- Maintain a minimum of `space-xl` (`2.5rem`) between disparate functional modules to prevent visual clutter.
- Cards encapsulate related interactive blocks with `space-lg` (`1.5rem`) internal padding.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy rejects stark drop-shadows and dark outlines. Elevation uses light, colored ambient diffusion combined with tonal background tiers.

### Depth System
- **Level 0 (Base Canvas):** Warm Parchment (`#FAFAF9`).
- **Level 1 (Default Containers & Feed Cards):** Pure White (`#FFFFFF`) with a 1px composite border (`rgba(30, 41, 59, 0.06)`) and an ultra-soft botanical ambient shadow: `0 4px 20px -2px rgba(46, 125, 91, 0.05)`.
- **Level 2 (Active Cards, Tele-consultation Drawers, Floating Controls):** Pure White (`#FFFFFF`) supported by `0 12px 32px -4px rgba(30, 41, 59, 0.08)`.
- **Level 3 (Crisis Popovers & Emergency Drawers):** High-priority focus layered above a tinted backdrop overlay (`rgba(30, 41, 59, 0.4)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(8px)`) using a gentle coral ambient halo: `0 20px 40px -8px rgba(225, 29, 72, 0.12)`.

## Shapes

The design uses a generous curved corner language (`roundedness: 2`) that conveys warmth, safety, and physical gentleness.

### Curvature Tokens
- **Base Components (Inputs, Small Badges, Chips):** `0.5rem` (8px) to `0.75rem` (12px) for sharp interaction targets.
- **Cards & Primary Modules:** `1rem` (16px) for content items; `1.5rem` (24px) for hero containers, counselor spotlight profile cards, and video consultation viewports.
- **Pills & Status Containers:** Fully rounded (`9999px`) for counselor availability indicators, modal triggers, and anonymity shields.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Background `#2E7D5B`, text `#FFFFFF`, border-radius `9999px` (pill), height `48px`, font `label-lg`. Hover shifts to `#246549` with a subtle elevation lift.
- **Secondary / Supportive:** Background `#F3EFFF`, text `#7C69AF`, border-radius `9999px`.
- **SOS Emergency:** Background `#E11D48`, text `#FFFFFF`, border-radius `9999px`. Prominent, unmistakable, and permanently accessible in student navigation bars.

### Input Fields & Reflection Prompts
- Background `#FFFFFF` encased in a 1px border (`#E2E8F0`). Height `52px` with `16px` horizontal padding. Focus state switches border to `#2E7D5B` accompanied by a `0 0 0 3px #EBF5F0` glow. Placeholder text in `#94A3B8`.

### Checkboxes & Radio Selectors
- Softly rounded checks (`6px`) and circular radios (`50%`). In active states, filled with `#2E7D5B` showing a white checkmark or center pip. Never use high-contrast angular markers.

### Status Badges & Specialized System Indicators
- **Counselor Availability Badge:** Fully rounded pill containing a 6px status dot.
  - *Available Now:* Background `#EBF5F0`, text `#2E7D5B`, dot `#2E7D5B`.
  - *In Session:* Background `#FFF7ED`, text `#C2410C`, dot `#EA580C`.
- **Mode Indicators (Chat, Voice, Video):** Subtle segmented buttons or chips framed with `8px` radius. Active mode is indicated with Lavender fill (`#F3EFFF`) and `#7C69AF` icon/typography.
- **Anonymity Shield:** A distinct component combining a shield icon and customizable pseudonym tag. Rendered on `#F1F5F9` background with `#475569` text, giving students absolute clarity on when identity obfuscation is active.

### Cards
- Counselor profile cards, session bookings, and journaling prompts sit on pure white containers with `16px` or `24px` radii. Dividers between session notes use delicate borders (`rgba(30, 41, 59, 0.05)`).
